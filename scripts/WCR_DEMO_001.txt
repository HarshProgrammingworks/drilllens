WELL CONTROL REPORT WCR_DEMO_001
Source type: DEMO
Well: Barmer Bhagyam 02
Well ID: WL-IN-002
Field: Bhagyam
Operator: Cairn Oil & Gas
Report type: WCR
Date: 2024-03-12

This demonstration report is stored so the extraction pipeline can be tested on Indian onshore wells.
It reflects real drilling incident patterns from the Barmer Basin, Rajasthan.

At 2450 m, torque increased significantly while drilling Barmer Hill Formation and a stuck-pipe indicator was recorded, WOB was reduced and torque stabilized.

At 2380 m, standpipe pressure increased rapidly and a kick was suspected in the Barmer Hill Formation, the well was shut in and pressure stabilized.

At 2510 m, mud flow decreased and pump pressure dropped in the Dharvi Dungar Formation and lost circulation was observed, LCM was pumped and circulation was partially restored.

At 2600 m, gas-cut mud was reported in the Fatehgarh Formation and mud weight was 1.22 sg.

At 2320 m, a cementing problem was recorded after the casing cement job in the Thumbli Formation, and the cement job completed.

Equipment noted: PDC bit, BHA, jars, and casing.
Drilling parameter: WOB was 14 klbf. RPM was 110. ROP was 16.
