Source type: DEMO
Well: North Rift 2
Well ID: WL-002
Field: North Rift
Operator: Rift Energy
Report type: WCR
Date: 2024-03-12

This demonstration report is stored so the extraction pipeline can be tested.
It is not a record from an operating rig.

At 2450 m, torque increased significantly while drilling Wolfcamp Formation and a stuck-pipe indicator was recorded, WOB was reduced and torque stabilized.

At 2380 m, standpipe pressure increased rapidly and a kick was suspected in the Wolfcamp Formation, the well was shut in and pressure stabilized.

At 2510 m, mud flow decreased and pump pressure dropped in the Bone Spring Formation and lost circulation was observed, LCM was pumped and circulation was partially restored.

At 2600 m, gas-cut mud was reported in the Bone Spring Formation and mud weight was 1.22 sg.

At 2320 m, a cementing problem was recorded after the casing cement job in the Spraberry Formation, and the cement job completed.

Equipment noted: PDC bit, BHA, jars, and casing.
Drilling parameter: WOB was 14 klbf. RPM was 110. ROP was 16.
